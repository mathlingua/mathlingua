== begin:description ==
Accepts direct member access supplied by a capability on the owner's nominal type.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\set]
Declares: X
Requires:
. capability: x_ "in" X :-> \\abstract
Appendix:
. called: "set"

[\element:of{X}]
Declares: x "in" X
when: X is \set
Enables:
. capability: x.self :=> x
  written: "x?"
Appendix:
. called: "element"

Theorem:
given:
. X is \set
. p is \element:of{X}
then: p.self is? \element:of{X}
