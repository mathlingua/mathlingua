== begin:description ==
Rejects direct `is` and quoted-operator specifications for the same subject in one when scope.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\set]
Declares: X
Requires:
. capability: x_ "in" X :-> \\abstract
Documented:
. called: "set"

[\bad{A}:in{B}]
Declares: X
when:
. B is \set
. A is \set
. A "in" B
Documented:
. called: "bad"
== end:input ==
== begin:output ==
error: Subject `A` is specified more than once in the same `when:` scope (`is` and `quoted specification`); choose exactly one of `is`, a quoted specification, a spec-infix specification, or `:=`
== end:output ==
