== begin:description ==
Rejects direct `is` and spec-infix specifications for the same subject in one when scope.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\set]
Declares: X
Documented:
. called: "set"

[A \:subset:/ B]
Declares: A is \set
when: B is \set
Documented:
. called: "subset"

[\bad{A}:in{B}]
Declares: X
when:
. B is \set
. A is \set
. A \:subset:/ B
Documented:
. called: "bad"
== end:input ==
== begin:output ==
error: Subject `A` is specified more than once in the same `when:` scope (`is` and `spec-infix specification`); choose exactly one of `is`, a quoted specification, a spec-infix specification, or `:=`
== end:output ==
