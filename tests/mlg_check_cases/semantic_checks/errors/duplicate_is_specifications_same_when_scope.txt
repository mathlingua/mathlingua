== begin:description ==
Rejects two direct `is` specifications for the same subject in one when scope.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\bad{A}]
Declares: X
when:
. A is \\anything
. A is \\anything
Documented:
. called: "bad"
== end:input ==
== begin:output ==
error: Subject `A` is specified more than once in the same `when:` scope (`is` and `is`); choose exactly one of `is`, a quoted specification, a spec-infix specification, or `:=`
== end:output ==
