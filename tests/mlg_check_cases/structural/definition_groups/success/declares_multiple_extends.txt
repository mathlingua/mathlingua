== begin:description ==
Accepts two independent supertype declarations in an extends section.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\first.type]
Declares: x
Documented:
. called: "first type"

[\second.type]
Declares: x
Documented:
. called: "second type"

[\child]
Declares: x
extends:
. x is \first.type
. x is \second.type
Documented:
. called: "child"
