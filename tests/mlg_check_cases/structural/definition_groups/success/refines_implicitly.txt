== begin:description ==
Accepts an implicitly marked Refines item that names the corresponding refinement of its supertype.
== end:description ==
== begin:input ==
[\base]
Declares: x
Documented:
. called: "base"

[\child]
Declares: x is \base
Documented:
. called: "child"

[\(special)::base]
Refines: x
satisfies: x is \base
Documented:
. adjective: "special"

[\(special)::child]
Refines: x
implicitly:
specifies: x is \(special)::base
Documented:
. adjective: "special"
== end:input ==
